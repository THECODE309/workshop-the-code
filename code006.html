THE STORY

At some point in life, we understand that nobody is going to do it for us. Our decisions are ours. Our mistakes are ours. And so are the consequences. Responsibility is not about being perfect. It is about having the courage to recognise when we were wrong, to correct what we can and to keep moving forward. It is about keeping your word. Doing what you said you would do. Looking after the people who depend on you. And understanding that everything we do leaves a mark. CODE 006 is about taking ownership. Of our work. Of our choices. Of our mistakes. And ultimately, of our own story. Because nobody else can live it for us.
