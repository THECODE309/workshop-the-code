THE STORY

Respect is one of the first things we learn. But understanding it takes much longer. We learn that respect is not about position, money or status. It is about how you treat the person who can give you nothing in return. It is about listening before judging. About recognising the work of others. About knowing where you stand without believing you stand above anyone else. Respect for the person beside you. Respect for the work in front of you. Respect for the word you give. And, perhaps most importantly, respect for yourself. Because the way we treat others says more about who we are than anything we can say about ourselves. CODE 007 is about that principle. Respect is earned. Respect is given. And it always starts with us.
